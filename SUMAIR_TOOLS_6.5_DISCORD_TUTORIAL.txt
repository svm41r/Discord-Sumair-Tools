2. 2-MINUTE INSTALLATION GUIDE
================================================================================

[Option A: 1-Click Automated Installer (Recommended for Windows)]
1. Download "SumairTools_v6.5.zip" from https://sumairtools.online/ and extract it.
2. Right-click "Fix_AfterEffects_Extension.bat" and select "Run as Administrator".
3. The script will automatically:
   - Close any running After Effects / CEPHtmlEngine processes.
   - Enable PlayerDebugMode across all CSXS registry keys (CSXS.4 to CSXS.20).
   - Cleanly deploy the extension to your user & system CEP directories.
4. Launch Adobe After Effects and navigate to:
   Window > Extensions > Sumair Tools

[Option B: ZXP Installer (Universal)]
1. Download "SumairTools_v6.5.zxp" from https://sumairtools.online/
2. Open ZXP Installer (https://zxpinstaller.com/) or aescripts ZXP Manager.
3. Drag & drop "SumairTools_v6.5.zxp" into the installer.
4. Launch After Effects -> Window > Extensions > Sumair Tools.

[Option C: Manual Zip Method]
1. Extract "SumairTools_v6.5.zip".
2. Press [Win + R], type: %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions  and hit Enter.
3. Copy the "Sumair Tools Extension" folder into this directory and rename it to:
   SumairTools
4. Enable PlayerDebugMode in Windows Registry (HKCU\Software\Adobe\CSXS.10/11/12 -> 
   String PlayerDebugMode = "1").
5. Launch After Effects -> Window > Extensions > Sumair Tools.

* PRO TIP: To reload the extension panel without restarting After Effects, click the
  "v6.5 🔄" version tag in the top-right corner of the panel header!

================================================================================

[Sub-Tab 1: Quick Transcribe (Whisper AI Engine)]
Automate speech-to-text directly from your timeline cuts!
1. Audio Extraction:
   - If you made cuts/edits on your timeline, click "🎙️ Comp to WAV". 
     This renders active timeline cuts into a 16-bit WAV file so your subtitles 
     match your cuts 100% with zero drift!
   - Or select an audio/video layer and click "🎯 Grab Layer", or click "📁 Browse".
2. Words Per Layer:
   - Select "🔥 2x" (Reels / TikTok / Shorts) or "⚡ 4x" (Standard Short-Form).
3. Transcribe:
   - Click "⚡ Transcribe & Export SRT". Whisper AI generates "captions.srt" in your 
     Downloads folder.
4. Build in After Effects:
   - Click "🎬 Build AE" to automatically generate synchronized subtitle layers!

[Sub-Tab 2: Normal Captions (The Priority #1 Pipeline)]
The legendary 1-click automated subtitle styling pipeline:
1. "⚡ AUTO PROCESS ALL CAPTIONS":
   Automatically runs: 1. SRT Import -> 2. Clean Punctuation -> 3. 2x/4x Line Break 
   -> 4. Caption Split -> 5. Apply Styling Presets.
2. "🔗 Fix Merge Caption & Extend Rails":
   Repairs overlapping captions and extends dual-rail adjustment layers comp-wide.
3. "💥 Explode Caption Words":
   Explodes single text layers into individual word layers (Horizontal or Vertical).
4. "⏶ Typography Shifter":
   Use "▲ Shift Word Up" or "▼ Shift Word Down" to move words between lines without retyping.
5. "✨ Text Animation Presets":
   Apply viral presets in 1-click: TextOpac, TextUp, TextDown, Sweap, 3/4 Words Red.
6. "🧹 Sanitize & Cleanup":

6. MODULE 3: AUTO CUT (SILENCE REMOVAL & BEAT-SYNC)
================================================================================

[Mode 1: 🎙️ Silence Removal]
1. Select your audio/video layer in the timeline.
2. Set your parameters:
   - Silence Threshold: e.g. -40 dB
   - Min Silence Duration: e.g. 0.5 sec
   - Clip Padding: e.g. 8 frames (guard frames to prevent clipped syllables)
   - Audio Crossfades: Keep ON (2 frames) to eliminate audio pops and clicks.
3. Click "✂️ AUTO-CUT TIMELINE". Pauses are sliced and removed automatically!

[Mode 2: 🎵 Beat Markers]
1. Select your music track layer.
2. Set Peak Sensitivity (1-10) and Min Beat Gap (sec).
3. Click "✂️ MARK BEATS" to drop timeline markers on the highest musical beats!

10. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ
================================================================================

Q1: The extension panel is blank / white screen.
Fix: Run "Fix_AfterEffects_Extension.bat" as Administrator. This sets PlayerDebugMode=1
     in the Windows Registry so unsigned extensions load properly.

Q2: My subtitles don't match my audio cuts.
Fix: Click "🎙️ Comp to WAV" in Quick Transcribe. This renders your exact timeline cuts
     to WAV so Whisper AI transcribes the edited audio rather than the raw file.

Q3: How do I reload the extension without restarting After Effects?
Fix: Click the "v6.5 🔄" tag in the top-right header of the extension!

Q4: Machine Mismatch error on license activation.
Fix: License keys are locked to 1 PC HWID. Message Sumair on Discord if you switched
     PCs or reinstalled Windows to reset your hardware binding.
